# Store assets

App Store marketing screenshots for the iPhone 6.9" size class (1320×2868, `iphone-6.9/S1–S6.png`), the 1024×1024 in-app-purchase review image for the Scan Pack consumables (`scanpack-review-1024.png`), a one-row contact sheet of the six frames (`preview.png`), and the licensed house photo used in the S5 property header together with its Pexels License proof (`house-photo/`). Frames were captured from the real app in the iOS Simulator (dark appearance, 9:41 status bar) on the demo account and composed with Helvetica Neue Bold captions, a device-style frame, and a per-vertical accent glow; the app is iPhone-only (`supportsTablet: false`), so no iPad set is kept.

Caption order:

1. S1 — Dashboard, Your Month Ahead: "Your car, home & health. / One app."
2. S2 — Vehicle schedule with cost estimates: "Your maintenance plan / in 30 seconds"
3. S3 — Receipt-scan result (auto-filled Log Service): "Scan a receipt. / We log the rest."
4. S4 — Voice log outcome (parsed confirm card): "Say it. / It's logged."
5. S5 — Home tasks with property photo: "Know what your home / needs next"
6. S6 — Pet (Bella) health schedule: "Never miss / a vet visit"

Demo / App Review account: `appreview@lifemaintained.com` (seeded with a 2019 Toyota 4Runner, a Grayslake IL house, and family members Sam and Bella). The password is **not** in this repo; it lives locally in `.secrets/appreview-credentials.txt` (git-ignored).
